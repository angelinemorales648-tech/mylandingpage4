About Our System

Technology that supports better fishpond monitoring

The IoT-Enabled Water Quality Monitoring System for Nile Tilapia Aquaculture is designed to help fish farmers monitor important water-quality parameters. It collects sensor readings and presents the information through a web-based dashboard. SMS notifications can alert users when readings reach the configured warning levels, helping them decide when pond conditions need closer attention.

Our Key Features

Important water-quality information in one system

🌡️

Temperature Monitoring

Observe water temperature and identify readings that reach the system's configured warning levels.

🧪

pH Monitoring

View water pH readings to help assess whether pond water is within the selected reference range.

💧

Turbidity Monitoring

Observe water cloudiness through turbidity readings and identify conditions that may need further checking.

📊

Web-Based Dashboard

View collected readings and available recorded data through an organized dashboard.

📱

SMS Notifications

🐟

Tilapia Pond Support

How It Works

From sensor readings to useful information

1

Monitor

Sensors collect temperature, pH, and turbidity readings from the pond.

2

Process

The microcontroller processes the collected readings.

3

Transmit

The system sends available data to the web dashboard for viewing and recording.

4

Alert

The system sends an SMS notification when a configured alert condition is detected.
